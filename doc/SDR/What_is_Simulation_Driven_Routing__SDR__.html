What is Simulation Driven Routing (SDR)?

什么是仿真驱动布线（SDR）？

The Virtuoso simulation-driven routing (SDR) solution is a step towards correct-by-construction routing driven by electrical requirements. It provides an environment to consider current density and maximum resistance design rules and automatically resizes wires and vias during interactive routing.
The key features of Virtuoso simulation-driven routing let you:

Virtuoso 仿真驱动布线（SDR）方案是朝向“正确构建（correct-by-construction）”布线的一步，该方案由电气需求驱动。它提供一个环境，用于考虑电流密度与最大电阻等设计规则，并在交互式布线过程中自动调整导线与过孔的尺寸。
Virtuoso 仿真驱动布线的关键特性使你能够：

  • visualize the current distribution per net.
  • 按网络可视化电流分布。
  • control simulation-driven routing to calculate the current according to the net topology.
  • 控制仿真驱动布线根据网络拓扑计算电流。
  • automatically resize wires and vias according to the estimated current.
  • 根据估算电流自动调整导线与过孔尺寸。
  • automatically connect devices according to the estimated current.
  • 根据估算电流自动连接器件。
