How do I identify the net connections that either consume or generate the most current?

如何识别消耗或产生电流最多的网络连接？

Before starting interactive SDR routing, it is important to identify the net connections that either consume or generate the most current. Identifying the main current consumers and producers helps you to estimate the wire widths and vias to create an EM-compliant topology.
To visualize the pins of a net according to their current distribution:

在开始交互式 SDR 布线之前，识别哪些网络连接消耗或产生最多电流非常重要。找出主要的电流“源”和“汇”有助于你估算导线线宽与过孔，从而创建满足 EM（电迁移）要求的拓扑结构。
要根据电流分布可视化网络的引脚：

  1. Select a net from the Navigator assistant or EAD Browser.
  2. Click Hide Sources and Sinks Map on SDR Toolbar.

The layout canvas displays the group of abutted instances of the selected net. The colored halo around the grouped instances indicates whether it is a current consumer or a producer. The pins or instance pins producing the current are shown with a blue halo, and the sinks, pins, or instance pins consuming the current are shown with a red halo. The dark blue and dark red indicate higher current; lighter colors indicate a lower current.

  1. 在 Navigator assistant 或 EAD Browser 中选择一个网络。
  2. 在 SDR 工具栏点击 Hide Sources and Sinks Map。

版图画布会显示所选网络中相互贴靠（abutted）的实例分组。分组实例周围的彩色光晕表示其是电流的消耗者还是产生者：产生电流的引脚或实例引脚以蓝色光晕显示；消耗电流的汇（sinks）、引脚或实例引脚以红色光晕显示。深蓝与深红表示更高电流；颜色越浅表示电流越低。

Sometimes, you are unable to view the current value of the cluster. In this case, you can zoom in to the cluster and view the current value.

有时你可能无法查看该簇（cluster）的电流数值。此时可以放大到该簇以查看电流值。
