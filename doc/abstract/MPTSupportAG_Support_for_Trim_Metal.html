Support for Trim Metal

Trim Metal 支持

Trim metal, also referred to as cut metal, is a physical layer used to trim portions of a metal layer to achieve tight end-to-end line spacing. Trim metals are defined in the technology file. They can be assigned to cut-colored (masks) or non-colored metal geometries.

Trim metal（也称 cut metal）是一种物理层，用于裁切金属层的部分区域，以实现更紧的端到端线间距。Trim metal 在工艺文件中定义，可分配给 cut-colored（mask）或非着色的金属几何图形。

A trim metal trims a specific color (mask) of metal in the layout. For example, TM_1 trims Metal2:mask1 and TM_2 trims Metal2:mask2. Therefore, in situations where a trim metal cuts multiple metal layers, only a specific metal layer is trimmed. Please note that metal layers are cut during fabrication.

Trim metal 会裁切版图中某一特定颜色（mask）的金属。例如，TM_1 裁切 Metal2:mask1，TM_2 裁切 Metal2:mask2。因此，当某个 trim metal 对多层金属产生裁切时，实际上只会裁切某一特定金属层。请注意：金属层是在制造过程中被裁切的。

In the following diagrams, TM_1 is a trim metal that cuts only (Metal1 mask1); and TM_2 cuts only (Metal1 mask2). These trim metals:

在下图中，TM_1 仅裁切 (Metal1 mask1)；TM_2 仅裁切 (Metal1 mask2)。这些 trim metal：

  • Do not have any effect on the same metal shapes drawn on other masks
    不会影响绘制在其他 mask 上的同层金属图形
  • Do not affect any other metal
    不会影响任何其他金属

In the first figure, there are continuous metal lines in the layout. These shapes are cut into two parts by trim metals, which can be connected to different nets, as shown in the second figure.

在第一幅图中，版图里存在连续的金属线。这些图形会被 trim metal 裁切为两部分，并可按第二幅图所示连接到不同的 net。

Trim metals can be assigned to:

Trim metal 可分配给：

  • Non–colored (gray) metal geometries: You can specify the color of the metal layer to be trimmed.
    非着色（灰色）金属几何图形：您可以指定要被裁切的金属层颜色。
  • Cut-Colored metal geometries: Trims metal layers of the same color as the trim metal.
    Cut-Colored 金属几何图形：裁切与该 trim metal 同色的金属层。

Abstract Generator supports trim metals. While loading a library, Abstract Generator automatically fetches information about trim metals from the technology file. Trim metals are recognized at each step of the abstract generation process. Therefore, at each step, metal layers are considered as effective after they are cut by trim metals in the previous step. Only metal shapes are cut by trim metals.

抽象生成器支持 trim metal。加载库时，抽象生成器会自动从工艺文件获取 trim metal 信息。抽象生成过程的每一步都会识别 trim metal。因此，在每个步骤中，金属层会在上一阶段被 trim metal 裁切后才被视为有效。仅金属图形会被 trim metal 裁切。

Abstract Generator supports coloring for all metal layer shapes up to Mask8 and supports coloring for trim layers up to mask3.

抽象生成器支持对最高至 Mask8 的所有金属层图形进行着色，并支持对最高至 mask3 的 trim 层进行着色。
