Restrictions for Signal and Power Pin Geometry

信号与电源引脚几何形状的限制

The following restrictions apply to setting signal and power pin geometry:

设置信号与电源引脚几何形状时需遵循以下限制：

  • If there is a metal0 local interconnect layer, it would have to be entered manually.
  • 如果存在 metal0 本地互连层，则必须手动输入该层。
  • If two layers connect by overlap, specify a layer pair (M0 M1).
  • 如果两层通过重叠连接，请指定层对 (M0 M1)。
  • It is not permitted to specify (layer1 layer2 via12) and (layer1 layer2) and so on at the same time.
  • 不允许同时指定 (layer1 layer2 via12) 与 (layer1 layer2) 等组合。

For example,

例如：

(metal1 metal2 via12) (metal2 metal3 via23)
(poly metal1 cont)
(pdiff metal1 cont)
(ndiff metal1 cont)
(poly li)
(ndiff li)
(pdiff li)

This means,

其含义为：

  • metal1 connects to metal2 through via12 layer
  • metal1 通过 via12 层与 metal2 连接
  • metal2 connects to metal3 through via23 layer
  • metal2 通过 via23 层与 metal3 连接
  • poly connects to metal1 through cont layer
  • poly 通过 cont 层与 metal1 连接
  • pdiff connects to metal1 through cont layer
  • pdiff 通过 cont 层与 metal1 连接
  • ndiff connects to metal1 through cont layer
  • ndiff 通过 cont 层与 metal1 连接
  • poly connects to local interconnect by overlap
  • poly 通过重叠与本地互连连接
  • ndiff connects to local interconnect by overlap
  • ndiff 通过重叠与本地互连连接
  • pdiff connects to local interconnect by overlap
  • pdiff 通过重叠与本地互连连接
