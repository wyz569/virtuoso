How do I know if the pins have been successfully assigned?

如何确认引脚是否已成功分配？

While assigning pins, whenever you click OK in the Pin Assignments section, it checks whether all the enabled pins have been assigned stimuli. If unassigned pins are found, a warning message is generated suggesting that there are incomplete stimuli assignments in the design, which can lead to errors while generating a netlist. Also, there are signs representing warning or success near Pins or Globals in the Pins Assignment toolbar.

在分配引脚时，每当你在 Pin Assignments 部分单击 OK，它都会检查所有已启用的引脚是否已分配激励。如果发现未分配的引脚，会生成警告信息，提示设计中存在不完整的激励分配，这可能在生成网表时导致错误。此外，在 Pins Assignment 工具栏中，Pins 或 Globals 旁边会显示表示警告或成功的图标。

  • Pins: A warning icon appears when there are enabled pins in the design that do not have assigned stimuli . When all pins are assigned stimuli, a green tick appears next to pins . 
    Pins：当设计中存在已启用但未分配激励的引脚时，会出现警告图标 。当所有引脚都已分配激励时，Pins 旁会出现绿色勾选 。
  • Globals: A warning icon appears when there are enabled global sources in the design that do not have assigned stimuli .
    Globals：当设计中存在已启用但未分配激励的全局源时，会出现警告图标 。

The Pins Assignment section shows the total number of pins assigned / total number of pins in the design. It also indicates the number of disabled pins. These numbers also include the bus bits in your design.
Pins Assignment 部分显示 total number of pins assigned / total number of pins in the design。它还会指示已禁用引脚的数量。这些数字也包含设计中的总线位。
